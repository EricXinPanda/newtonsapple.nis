About Newton's Apple

A student-led STEM publication and innovation platform at NIS Chiang Mai.

Why Newton's Apple?

The falling apple is more than a story about gravity. It represents a question becoming an idea, and an idea becoming something that can be tested. Newton's Apple exists to make that process visible: students ask, investigate, build, explain and share.

Our work spans research projects, technical articles, magazine features and public STEM Talks. The goal is not to imitate a professional journal, but to practise the habits that make scientific communication valuable: clarity, evidence, intellectual curiosity and responsible uncertainty.

CuriosityStart with questions worth investigating.
EvidenceSeparate claims from assumptions and support conclusions with data or reasoning.
ClarityMake technical ideas understandable without oversimplifying them.
CommunityConnect student STEM work to NIS, Chiang Mai and the world beyond school.
